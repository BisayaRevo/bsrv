OUR STANDARD

COMMUNITY RULES

Respect the community. Protect its reputation.

I. RESPECT & CONDUCT

Treat every member with respect. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and toxic behavior are not tolerated.

II. SERVER ETIQUETTE

Follow Discord server rules, use channels appropriately, and avoid spam or disruptive behavior.

III. CLAN LOYALTY

Represent BISAYAREVO responsibly. Do not intentionally damage the clan's reputation or create unnecessary conflicts.

IV. BUILDERS & REDSTONERS

Respect other people's builds and creations. Do not destroy, steal, or interfere with another member's work without permission.

V. STAFF & HIERARCHY

Respect staff decisions and follow the proper hierarchy. Concerns should be raised through the appropriate channels.

VI. COMMUNICATION

Keep communication clear and respectful. Avoid unnecessary arguments, spam, and misuse of community channels.

VII. ENFORCEMENT

Rule violations may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans, depending on severity.

VIII. APPEALS

Members may appeal moderation decisions through the official ticket system. Be respectful and provide relevant information.
