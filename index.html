04 / CODE OF CONDUCT

OFFICIAL RULES

Every member is expected to understand and follow the standards of BISAYAREVO.

I. RESPECT & CONDUCT

Treat every member with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and unnecessary hostility are not accepted.

II. SERVER ETIQUETTE

Follow server rules, avoid spam, and keep discussions appropriate and organized.

III. CLAN LOYALTY

Represent BISAYAREVO responsibly. Do not misuse the clan name or intentionally damage its reputation.

IV. BUILDERS & REDSTONERS

Respect other members' creations. Do not destroy, steal, or interfere with another member's work.

V. STAFF & HIERARCHY

Respect the staff structure. Follow reasonable instructions and use the proper channels for concerns.

VI. COMMUNICATION

Keep communication clear and respectful. Use the correct channels for their intended purposes.

VII. ENFORCEMENT

Violations may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans. Appeals can be submitted through the ticket system.
